Patrick of Better On Foot was one of the first artists to remix a Private Agenda track, and one of the first connections we made in the US when we released our first two records on International Feel.

We've never had the pleasure of meeting in person, but our collaboration has stood the test of time, thanks to our shared love of the warm, optimistic and melodic dance music he has always produced and championed. We started 2026 with this remix of Limerence by Fuchsia Casual on his label, Better On Foot. The musical friendship stretches back into our past, but the track also has elements of Private Agenda's future.

Over the summer, the remix was in rotation at Soho House locations around the world, from São Paulo to Bangkok, Toronto and Rome, via their Poolside playlist.

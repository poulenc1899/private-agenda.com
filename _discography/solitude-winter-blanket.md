Solitude, from our debut album [Île de Rêve](/discography/Ile-de-Reve/), was included on Winter Blanket, Lo Recordings' compilation for the winter of 2020.

A few months later, Delac remixed it for [Submersion](/discography/submersion/), our album of Île de Rêve remixes.

New year, new remix: a dreamy collaboration with Tokyo Tea Room on their track If You Love Her. Picture yourself by the water as the sun sets, casting a warm glow across the landscape. Can you hear the ebb and flow of gentle waves?

For long-time Private Agenda listeners, this one reunited us with Gautier of Nice Guys, who we'd worked with on our soft-yet-cinematic remix of [Irréversible](/discography/Irreversible/) back in 2018. The remix also appears on Tokyo Tea Room's No Future Plans (Remixes).

'Suprasensual' sounds like getting in the car, throwing away the sat nav, winding the windows down and turning the radio up as far as it'll go. You don't care where you're going, or how long it takes to get there: it just feels amazing.

We remember driving around our hometown, High Wycombe, together when we first got our licences. There was so little public transport where we grew up, so it was the first time we'd been able to decide for ourselves when, where and how fast to go. That felt like the ultimate freedom.

'Suprasensual' is the lead single and first glimpse of our new album, [Summon](/discography/summon/), out 6th February 2027 on Nightshift.

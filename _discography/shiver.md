You know those tracks that don't just make you a little emotional, but have a physical effect too? That was our inspiration for Shiver: to make a song that feels like a warm embrace.

It's the third single from Presence, our "go-with-your-gut" singles series, after [Presence](/discography/presence/) and [Blueprints](/discography/blueprints/). Like every track in the series, it has collaboration at its core. Esther Hayes whispers softly on the chorus, Nat Reading crafts a sensual spine on bass and electric guitar, and Pete adds some deft and delicate percussion.

We were delighted to sing on Young Men Drive Fast, the album from Y.M.D.F., a collaboration between Ilija Rudman and Joe Pandur. Our vocal version of Southbound Cruising is the album's bonus track.

The album came out on NuNorthern Soul as a limited-edition vinyl. Paul Carr masterfully engineered the vocals.

Narrowing is our collaboration with the experimental electronic two-piece Delmer Darion, released as a single on Practise Music. It also appears on their album Morning Pageants, released in October 2020.

Delmer Darion later remixed Dependency for [Submersion](/discography/submersion/), our album of Île de Rêve remixes.

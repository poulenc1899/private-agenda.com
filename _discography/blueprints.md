Blueprints is the second of three tracks from Presence, our "go-with-your-gut" singles series, released on our own imprint, Nightshift, in 2023.

[Presence](/discography/presence/), Blueprints and [Shiver](/discography/shiver/) all explore the feeling of being in the room with the music, creating a sonic space that invites you to immerse yourself in the sound.

That year we also took the Presence songs to the stage with our four-piece band, playing some of the best-sounding shows we've ever done, in London.

Dressed in Velvet was the second single from our album [A Mannequin](/discography/a-mannequin/), following [Neo-Nostalgia](/discography/neo-nostalgia/). It came out on Lo Recordings alongside the pre-order of the album on 12-inch white vinyl.

The official video asks a simple question: can you escape the mesmerising maze of modernity?
